# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want Dokimana to be a useful, welcoming project for people who care about careful API migrations and honest tooling. Participation is based on respect, not agreement.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct, specific, and civil.
- Assume good intent while naming concrete problems.
- Give reproducible details when reporting a defect.
- Respect different levels of experience with Stripe, TypeScript, JavaScript, and open source.
- Keep discussion tied to the code, docs, and project boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information without consent.
- Sharing live keys, tokens, customer data, or private specifications in public threads.
- Deliberate misrepresentation of what the tool can prove.
- Sustained disruption of issues, reviews, or other project spaces.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, and other project spaces where community members interact.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close disruptive threads, or block participation when needed. Report conduct problems through GitHub's private contact options when available. Security issues belong in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public issues.

## Attribution

This policy is written for Dokimana and reflects the project's boundary of evidence over proof.
